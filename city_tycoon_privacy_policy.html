This Privacy Policy explains how Ankit Greyrat Studios ("we", "us") handles information when you use the mobile game City Tycoon: Build Your Empire ("the Game"). By using the Game, you agree to this policy.

1. Information we collect

We do not ask you to create an account, and we do not ask for your name, phone number, or address to play the Game. However, the Game and the third-party services it uses may collect:

  • Device and usage information: device model, operating system version, language, app version, game settings, and in-game activity such as modes played and session length.

  • Diagnostics: crash reports and performance data that help us find and fix bugs.
  • Approximate location: a general location (such as country or region) derived from your IP address. We do not collect precise GPS location.

Your game progress and preferences are stored on your device.

The Game may use third-party services such as Google Play Services, Google Play Billing, Google AdMob, and Firebase (analytics and crash reporting). These services may collect information as described in their own privacy policies, including Google's at policies.google.com/privacy.

The Game is free to play and may show ads between matches. You can limit ad personalization or reset your advertising ID in your Android device settings (Settings > Google > Ads). Optional purchases, such as removing ads or buying cosmetic items, do not give any gameplay advantage.

The Game is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has given us personal information, please contact us and we will delete it.

Game data stored on your device is removed when you uninstall the Game. Information held by third-party services is kept according to their policies. To ask us to delete any information we hold about you, email us at the address below.
